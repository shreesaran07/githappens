Slide 2 Overview

Problem Statement

Structural vulnerabilities facing grassroots creators.

👁️

Digital Invisibility

Rural photographers and grassroots creators produce rich visual content that remains hidden from global markets due to a lack of platform access[cite: 1].

⚠️

Discovery & Verification Barriers

Traditional platforms rely on follower counts and filenames rather than quality, while AI manipulation makes authenticity hard to assess[cite: 1].

🔒

Licensing & Misuse Risks

Complex licensing rules and lack of copyright protection leave creators vulnerable to uncredited image reuse without monetization[cite: 1].

👥

Cultural Underrepresentation

Without dedicated discovery mechanisms, authentic local culture and rural stories remain digitally underrepresented[cite: 1].

Slide 3 Overview

Proposed Solution

End-to-end trust from visual feature inspection to smart contract execution.

✨

AI Aesthetic & Quality Curation

Computer vision models (CLIP, ViT) evaluate composition, lighting, and technical details to auto-generate quality scores and tags[cite: 1].

🛡️

Authenticity & AI Detection

Deep learning filters analyze manipulation signals, metadata, and asset fingerprints to provide an authenticity confidence score[cite: 1].

🪙

Decentralized Licensing

Smart contracts record ownership hashes and usage rights on-chain while keeping heavy image files in secure off-chain cloud storage[cite: 1].
